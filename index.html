APIx Trend

APIx Trend

Shows
The historical series of the prototype Indian Airfare Price Index, read from apix_history.
X-axis
Index date. Only dates that have an APIx value appear; missing dates are not filled or interpolated, the line just joins neighbouring points.
Y-axis
APIx value, a fixed-base index (100 = baseline date). The axis does not start at zero.
Periods
All shows the whole series. 30D and 7D keep index dates within 30 or 7 calendar days of the latest index date.
Caveat
Observed history, not a forecast. This chart does not respond to the filters on this page.

Historical movement of the Indian Airfare Price Index (prototype).

Route-wise Fare Ranking

Route-wise Fare Ranking

Shows
The median observed total fare for each route.
X-axis
Total fare in rupees (₹).
Y-axis
Route, origin to destination, sorted from highest to lowest median.
Basis
Observations of the latest collection date after the page filters. Only fares above zero are used.
Method
Median of all matching observations for the route, pooled across airlines, flights, travel dates and lead times.
Caveat
Route differences also reflect the mix of airlines and lead times observed. Click a bar to open the route detail.

Median observed total fare by route (₹).

Lead-time Elasticity

Lead-time Elasticity

Shows
The median observed total fare at each advance-purchase window.
X-axis
Lead time in days between collection and travel: T+1, T+7, T+15, T+30 and T+45.
Y-axis
Median total fare in rupees (₹). The axis does not start at zero.
Basis
Observations of the latest collection date after the page filters. Only fares above zero are used.
Caveat
This is a descriptive median per lead time, not a calculated price-elasticity coefficient. A lead time with no observations shows as a gap.

Median observed total fare (₹) across T+1 → T+45.

Airline Comparison

Airline Comparison

Shows
The median observed total fare for each airline.
X-axis
Airline, sorted from lowest to highest median.
Y-axis
Median total fare in rupees (₹), starting at zero. The tooltip also shows the observation count.
Basis
Observations of the latest collection date after the page filters. Only fares above zero are used.
Caveat
Fares are pooled across routes and lead times, so airlines that fly different routes are not strictly like-for-like. Use the Route filter to compare on a single route.

Median observed total fare (₹) by airline.

Observed Fare Distribution

Observed Fare Distribution

Shows
How many observed fares fall into each fare band (a histogram).
X-axis
Fare bands in rupees (₹), where k means thousand. Bands are equal width between the lowest and highest fare.
Y-axis
Number of observations in the band.
Basis
Observations of the latest collection date after the page filters. Only fares above zero are used.
Method
The number of bands is the square root of the observation count, kept between 5 and 10.
Caveat
All routes are pooled, so the shape largely reflects which routes and lead times are in the filtered view.

Number of observations per fare band.

Route Fare Volatility

Route Fare Volatility

Shows
How widely observed fares are spread within each route, relative to that route's mean fare.
Measure
Coefficient of variation: standard deviation divided by the mean, times 100. The standard deviation is the population form (divided by n).
X-axis
Coefficient of variation in percent.
Y-axis
Route, sorted from the most to the least dispersed.
Basis
Observations of the latest collection date after the page filters. Only fares above zero are used.
Caveat
This measures spread between observations collected on one date (airlines, flights, travel dates and lead times), not how prices move over time.

Relative dispersion of observed fares by route (coefficient of variation, %).

Methodology

How SkyIndex Works

01Collect

Airfare observations are collected for configured routes and travel dates.

02Clean

Invalid fares and incomplete observations are filtered before analysis.

03Represent

Median fare is used as the representative value for analytical views.

04Index

Route-level movements can be aggregated into the prototype APIx.

05Visualise

Charts, filters and the observation table expose the resulting data.

Prototype note

The current APIx implementation is an analytical prototype and should not be interpreted as an official government statistical methodology.
