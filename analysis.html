Fare change

Fare change

Shows
Percentage change in the median observed total fare between the first and the last collection date in the selected period.
Method
(last median ÷ first median − 1) × 100, using the median of all filtered observations on each of those two dates.
Caveat
Not a day-over-day change and not the APIx. Changes in the route or airline mix between the two dates also move the median.

Fare CV

Fare CV (coefficient of variation)

Shows
Spread of the selected observations relative to their mean fare.
Method
Sample standard deviation (divided by n − 1) over the mean, times 100, across every selected raw observation in the period.
Caveat
Pools routes, airlines, lead times and dates, so it measures dispersion across observations, not price volatility over time.

Observed fare trend

Observed fare trend

Shows
The median observed total fare on each real collection date.
X-axis
Collection date. Only dates that exist in the filtered data are plotted; nothing is filled in.
Y-axis
Median total fare in rupees (₹).
Basis
All observations that match the filters above.
Caveat
Observed history, not a forecast. The median pools routes, airlines and lead times, so a change in mix between dates can move it.

Lead-time behaviour

Shows
The representative fare at each advance-purchase window on the latest selected collection date.
X-axis
Lead time in days: T+1, T+7, T+15, T+30, T+45.

Fare composition

Fare composition

Shows
How the median of each fare component moves over the selected period.
X-axis
Collection date.
Y-axis
Median amount in rupees (₹) for base fare, taxes, UDF and fees.
Method
Each line is its own median per date, so the four lines do not necessarily add up to the median total fare. Other charges are not shown.
Caveat
A missing component value is counted as zero by the current calculation.

Base fare, taxes, UDF and fees over the selected period.
